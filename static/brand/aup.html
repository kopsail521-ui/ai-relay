Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Service: KeyoAPI

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) is part of our Terms of Service. It applies to all KeyoAPI accounts, API keys, and all AI-generated content — including text, images, audio, video, and voice.

Allowed use

  • Lawful software development, research, education, and business automation
  • Chat, coding assistance, and non-prohibited image generation through our API
  • Building products that clearly disclose KeyoAPI / third-party model usage where required

Prohibited use

You may not use KeyoAPI to create, request, host, or distribute:

  1. Sexual / NSFW content — pornography, erotic content, sexually suggestive imagery or chat, or “uncensored / no filter / NSFW” marketing claims
  2. Deepfakes & face manipulation — face-swap, non-consensual intimate imagery, or deceptive impersonation
  3. Child exploitation — any sexual or exploitative content involving minors
  4. Hate speech (仇恨言论) — AI-generated text, images, audio, video, or voice that demeans, threatens, harasses, or incites violence or discrimination against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, nationality, or other protected characteristics
  5. Violence & illegal harm — violent crime, terrorism, or illegal weapons
  6. Fraud & abuse — scams, phishing, malware, credential stuffing, spam
  7. IP & privacy violations
  8. Platform abuse — bypassing rate limits, filters, billing, or security controls
  9. Misrepresentation — claiming KeyoAPI is an official OpenAI / Anthropic / Google product

Enforcement

We may block or moderate prompts/outputs, rate-limit or terminate accounts, report illegal activity when appropriate, and refuse refunds where misuse caused suspension.
