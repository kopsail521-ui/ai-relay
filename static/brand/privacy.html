This Privacy Policy explains how KeyoAPI (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our OpenAI-compatible API gateway and related websites.

KeyoAPI provides API access to third-party AI models (chat and image generation) through a unified endpoint for developers. We are an independent service and are not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or other model providers.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Account data: username, email address, and authentication identifiers (including Google OIDC profile data if you sign in with Google)

  • Technical logs: basic server logs for security and reliability

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data beyond what is needed to operate the service.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the KeyoAPI service
  • Authenticate users and manage API keys
  • Process payments and allocate quota
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Prompt and generated content

API prompts and model outputs may transit our systems and upstream providers solely to fulfill your requests. We do not sell your prompts. Retention of prompt/content logs, if any, is limited to operational, billing, abuse-prevention, and legal needs.

You are responsible for ensuring that prompts and outputs comply with our Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy, and with applicable laws.

5. Sharing of information

We may share limited data with:

  • Payment processor / Merchant of Record: Creem (and its payment partners) for checkout, tax, and payouts

We do not sell personal information.
